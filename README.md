# Philben-files
Work for bioavailability paper with Philben

Primarily rscripts for making figures, but also a few for running PCAs, ANOVAs, multiple regressions, and stepwise regressions
Most scripts will have references at the top for wherever code was sourced from

Scripts key:

First paper submission code:
- "pca" & "pcastuff" are from April 2023
- "Respiration_R_Script.R", "GCMSTrialANOVA.R" & "Multiple regressions_peat bioavailability.R" are from February 2024
- "philben_pca_code.R", "philben_pca_code_copy.R" & "Peatbioavail_ANOVAs.R" are from November & December 2024
- "datatablecode.R" & "AAdatatablecode" are from August & September 2025

Revisions (second submission):
- "bioavailpaper_revisions_pcafigs.R", "bioavailpaper_revisions_fig1.R", "stepwiseregressions_paperrevisions.R" & "bioavailpaper_revisions_fig4.R" are from March & April 2026
- "test" is the file used for setting up and linking to GitHub, also has all the references
